# Project Catalogue

The complete 100-project catalogue is maintained in the root [README](README.md#project-catalogue).

The catalogue is divided into ten tracks:

1. AI for CI/CD and software delivery
2. AI for incident response and SRE
3. AI for observability and AIOps
4. AI for Kubernetes and cloud operations
5. AI for infrastructure as code and configuration
6. AI for DevSecOps and software supply chains
7. AI for platform engineering and developer experience
8. AI for FinOps, capacity, and performance
9. AI for reliability, resilience, and recovery
10. Enterprise AI operations capstones

Project identifiers and folder names are stable. A project title may change only when the change improves accuracy without changing the project's intended outcome.
