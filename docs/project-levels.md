# Project Levels

Level describes system complexity, not personal worth.

## Foundation

Typical scope:

- one main operational workflow;
- local or simulated environment;
- one bounded AI task;
- deterministic baseline;
- basic tests and CI;
- clear failure fallback.

Expected effort: 12–20 focused hours.

## Intermediate

Typical scope:

- two or more integrated components;
- external API or realistic platform interface;
- structured validation;
- security and operational telemetry;
- several controlled failures.

Expected effort: 20–35 focused hours.

## Advanced

Typical scope:

- cross-system data flow;
- asynchronous or event-driven behavior;
- policy and approval boundaries;
- measurable reliability and cost objectives;
- deployment and rollback considerations.

Expected effort: 30–50 focused hours.

## Senior Capstone

Typical scope:

- platform or organization-level problem;
- competing objectives and explicit trade-offs;
- multi-team ownership;
- production-style reliability, security and governance;
- decision records and operational review.

Expected effort: 40–70 focused hours.

## Level Rules

A higher level does not mean more documentation for its own sake. It means greater integration, uncertainty, operational consequence and engineering judgment.
