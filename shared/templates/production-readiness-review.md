# Production Readiness Review

## Service ownership

- [ ] Owners and escalation paths are documented.
- [ ] Dependencies and operational responsibilities are known.

## Reliability

- [ ] SLOs and failure behavior are defined.
- [ ] Backup, rollback, and recovery are tested.

## Security and AI safety

- [ ] Threat boundaries and permissions are reviewed.
- [ ] Inputs and outputs are validated.
- [ ] High-impact actions require approval.

## Operations

- [ ] Logs, metrics, traces, dashboards, and alerts exist.
- [ ] Runbooks and troubleshooting guidance are tested.

## Cost and capacity

- [ ] Expected cost and usage limits are documented.
- [ ] Load, scaling, and quota behavior are understood.
